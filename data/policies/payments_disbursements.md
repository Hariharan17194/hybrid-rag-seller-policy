# Payments and Disbursements

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an official Amazon policy. Numbers are illustrative.

This policy explains how and when sellers receive the money from their sales. Associates use it when a seller asks why a payment is late, why funds are on hold, or why the amount looks lower than expected.

## Settlement cycle

Seller payments are calculated in a settlement period of 14 days. At the end of each period, the available balance (sales minus fees, refunds and reserves) is sent to the seller's registered bank account. Sellers in good standing may request a disbursement once every 24 hours from the Payments dashboard.

## Bank transfer timelines

After a disbursement is initiated, funds normally reach the seller's bank account within 3 to 5 business days. Delays can be caused by bank holidays or incorrect bank details. If the bank rejects the transfer, the funds return to the seller's balance and the seller must update the bank account.

## Account level reserve

### Why a reserve is held

A reserve is part of the balance held back to cover possible refunds, A-to-z claims and chargebacks. New sellers and sellers with high claim rates may have a larger reserve.

### Delivery Date Based reserve

Under the delivery date based reserve, funds from an order become available 7 days after the confirmed delivery date. For orders without tracking, the estimated delivery date is used.

## Payment holds

Payments can be put on hold when the account is under review, when the deposit method is being verified, or when the account is deactivated. Funds held for a deactivated account are normally released 90 days after deactivation, minus any outstanding claims or refunds.

## Updating bank details

The seller updates the deposit method from the Settings page. For security, a newly added bank account cannot receive a disbursement for 3 days, and associates must never ask a seller to send bank details by email.
