# FBA Lost and Damaged Inventory Reimbursement

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an official Amazon policy. Numbers are illustrative.

When inventory sent to a fulfilment centre is lost or damaged while in the marketplace's control, the seller may be reimbursed. Associates use this policy for cases where a seller reports missing units, damaged units, or a reimbursement that looks too low.

## Eligible situations

- Units lost or damaged inside a fulfilment centre.
- Units lost or damaged during transport by a partnered carrier.
- Customer returns that were refunded but never returned to inventory within 45 days.

## Automatic reimbursement

Most lost or damaged units are reimbursed automatically. Before reimbursement, the system searches for misplaced units for up to 30 days, because units are often found during this time.

## Filing a claim

### Deadlines

If a seller believes a unit was not reimbursed automatically, they can file a claim within 60 days after the unit was marked lost or damaged. Shipment discrepancy claims (fewer units received than sent) must be filed within 15 to 60 days after the shipment was delivered.

### Evidence required

Proof of inventory ownership is needed, such as an invoice or manufacturer receipt, showing the product, quantity and supplier.

## Reimbursement value

The reimbursement value is based on an estimated sale price for the item, minus fees, and is capped at a maximum value per unit. Sellers can provide a manufacturing cost to influence the calculation for future reimbursements.
