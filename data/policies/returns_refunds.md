# Returns and Refunds for Seller-Fulfilled Orders

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an official Amazon policy. Numbers are illustrative.

This policy explains how sellers must handle return requests and refunds for orders they ship themselves. It does not apply to FBA orders, which are handled by the fulfilment network.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Some categories have different windows. Sellers may offer a longer window but not a shorter one.

## Prepaid return labels

Sellers must provide a prepaid return label for eligible returns, or authorise the marketplace to issue one. A seller who does not respond to a return request within 2 business days may have the request authorised automatically.

## Refund timelines

### Standard refunds

The seller must issue a refund within 2 business days after receiving the returned item. The refund goes to the buyer's original payment method.

### Restocking fees

A restocking fee of up to 20% may be charged only when the buyer returns an item that is opened, used, or missing parts, and the reason is not the seller's fault. Restocking fees cannot be charged when the item arrived damaged, defective, or not as described.

### Returnless refunds

Sellers can set rules to refund without requiring the item back, for example for low-value items where return shipping costs more than the item. Returnless refunds do not count as defects.

## Common seller questions

A seller who received a return in a different condition than shipped may file a SAFE-T claim within 30 days of the refund date. Associates should explain the evidence needed: photos of the returned item, the original listing condition, and the return tracking number.
