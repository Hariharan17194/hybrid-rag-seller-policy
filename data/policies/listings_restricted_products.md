# Listings and Restricted Products

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an official Amazon policy. Numbers are illustrative.

Some products can be sold by anyone, some need approval first, and some cannot be sold at all. Associates use this policy when a seller asks why a listing was suppressed or removed, or how to get approval to sell in a gated category.

## Product categories

### Open categories

Most categories, such as books, home and kitchen, and toys (outside the holiday season), are open. Any seller in good standing can list products in them.

### Gated categories (approval required)

Some categories need approval before the seller can list, for example jewellery, collectible coins, and certain grocery items. To request approval, the seller opens the "Add a product" page and selects "Request approval". Approval may require invoices from a manufacturer or distributor dated within the last 180 days, showing at least 10 units.

### Prohibited products

Prohibited products cannot be listed under any circumstances. Examples include recalled products, counterfeit goods, and items that make unapproved medical claims. A listing for a prohibited product is removed, and repeated attempts can lead to account deactivation.

## Suppressed listings

A listing is suppressed (hidden from search but not deleted) when required information is missing or invalid, for example a missing main image, a main image without a white background, a title longer than 200 characters, or a missing brand name. The seller fixes the listing from the "Fix your products" page, and the listing usually becomes active again within 24 hours.

## Listing policy violations

### Common violations

Common violations are keyword stuffing in the title, linking to external websites, promotional phrases such as "best price" or "free shipping" in the title, and creating a duplicate product page for an item that already has one.

### How to resolve a removed listing

The seller should edit the listing to remove the violation and then request a review from the Account Health page. Associates explain which field caused the removal but cannot manually reinstate a listing.
