# A-to-z Guarantee Claims

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an official Amazon policy. Numbers are illustrative.

The A-to-z Guarantee protects buyers when an item does not arrive or is materially different from the listing. Associates use this policy when a seller asks why a claim was granted, how to respond, or how to appeal a decision.

## Eligibility for buyers

A buyer can file a claim when:

- the item did not arrive within 3 calendar days after the maximum estimated delivery date, or
- the item arrived damaged, defective, or materially different from the listing, and the seller did not resolve a return request.

A buyer must first contact the seller and wait 48 hours before filing, unless the order shows as delivered but the buyer reports it missing.

## Seller response

### Response window

The seller has 72 hours to respond to a claim notification. If the seller does not respond, the claim may be decided in the buyer's favour and the amount debited from the seller.

### What a good response includes

Tracking with delivery confirmation, photos of the item before shipment, and any message history with the buyer. For orders above the signature threshold, proof of signature on delivery is required.

## Claims and Order Defect Rate

A claim that is granted counts as an order defect and affects ODR. A claim that is withdrawn by the buyer, or decided in the seller's favour, does not count. A claim granted only because the carrier lost the package, when the seller used a marketplace shipping service with valid tracking, is protected and does not affect ODR.

## Appealing a claim decision

The seller can appeal a claim decision within 30 calendar days of the decision. The appeal must include new information that was not available at the time of the original decision. Associates cannot reverse claim decisions; they guide the seller to the appeal option on the claim page.
