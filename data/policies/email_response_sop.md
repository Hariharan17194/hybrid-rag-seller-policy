# Associate Email Response SOP

> Synthetic standard operating procedure written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an internal Amazon or vendor document.

This SOP defines how Seller Support associates read, classify and answer seller emails.

## Step 1: Read and classify

Read the full email thread before replying. Classify the case into one primary category: Account Health, A-to-z Claim, Returns and Refunds, FBA Reimbursement, Listings, Payments, or Other. If the email covers two topics, answer both in one reply and classify by the main issue.

## Step 2: Verify the seller

Never disclose account details, payment amounts or buyer information unless the email comes from the registered account email address. If it does not, ask the seller to write in from the registered address.

## Step 3: Find the policy

Answer only from current, approved policy pages. Do not guess timelines or amounts. If no policy covers the question, escalate instead of improvising.

## Step 4: Write the reply

### Structure

1. Greet the seller by name.
2. Restate the issue in one sentence so the seller knows it was understood.
3. Give the answer or next steps in numbered points.
4. Link the relevant policy page.
5. Close politely and invite the seller to reply if anything is unclear.

### Tone

Be clear, polite and specific. Avoid internal jargon. Never promise an outcome that the associate cannot control, such as "your account will be reinstated".

## Step 5: Escalation

Escalate when: the seller reports a legal threat, a safety issue, suspected fraud, a request that needs a specialist team (such as a reinstatement decision), or the same issue has been answered three times without resolution.

## Quality metrics

Replies are reviewed for correct resolution, policy accuracy, tone, and response time. The target first response time is within 24 hours.
