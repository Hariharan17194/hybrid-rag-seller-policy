# Account Health and Order Defect Rate

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is modelled on how marketplace seller policies are structured. It is NOT an official Amazon policy. Numbers are illustrative.

Account Health measures whether a seller meets the marketplace's performance targets. Associates use this policy when a seller writes in about a warning, a deactivation, or a drop in their Account Health Rating (AHR).

## Order Defect Rate (ODR)

The Order Defect Rate is the percentage of orders with one or more defects in a 60-day window. A defect is a negative feedback (1 or 2 stars), an A-to-z Guarantee claim that was not denied, or a credit card chargeback.

### Target

Sellers must keep ODR under 1%. An ODR above 1% can lead to a warning, and a sustained ODR above 1% can lead to deactivation of selling privileges.

### How ODR is calculated

ODR = orders with at least one defect / total orders in the 60-day window. An order with both negative feedback and a chargeback counts as ONE defective order, not two.

## Late Shipment Rate (LSR)

Late Shipment Rate is the percentage of seller-fulfilled orders confirmed as shipped after the expected ship date. The target is under 4% over 10-day and 30-day windows. FBA orders do not count toward LSR.

## Pre-fulfillment Cancel Rate

Pre-fulfillment Cancel Rate is the percentage of seller-fulfilled orders cancelled by the seller before shipment. The target is under 2.5% over a 7-day window. Cancellations requested by the buyer through the order page are excluded.

## Deactivation and Appeals

### When an account is deactivated

An account can be deactivated when a performance metric stays above target, for policy violations, or for suspected inauthentic products. The seller receives an email that names the reason and links to the Account Health page.

### Plan of Action (POA)

To appeal, the seller submits a Plan of Action. A strong POA has three parts:

1. Root cause: what exactly caused the problem.
2. Corrective actions: what the seller already did to fix the affected orders.
3. Preventive measures: what process changes stop it from happening again.

Associates must not write the Plan of Action for the seller. Associates may explain the three parts and point the seller to the appeal page.

### Appeal timelines

A seller may submit an appeal within 17 days of the deactivation notice. If the first appeal is rejected, the seller may submit a revised appeal. There is no fixed limit on revised appeals, but each one must include new information. Reviews are usually completed within 48 hours.
