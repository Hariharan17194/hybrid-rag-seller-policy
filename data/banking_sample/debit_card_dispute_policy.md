# Debit Card Transaction Dispute Policy

> Synthetic training document written for this portfolio project. It is NOT an official policy of any bank. Numbers are illustrative.

This document shows that the SellerPolicy Assistant works with any policy library, not only marketplace policies. A bank's customer support team would use it to answer customer questions about disputed debit card transactions.

## Reporting an unauthorised transaction

Customers should report an unauthorised debit card transaction as soon as possible. If the customer reports within 3 working days of receiving the transaction alert, the customer's liability is zero. If the report is made between 4 and 7 working days, the customer's liability is capped at a fixed amount set in the schedule of charges.

## Blocking the card

The customer can block the card instantly through the mobile app, internet banking, or the 24x7 helpline. A replacement card is dispatched within 7 working days.

## Dispute investigation

### Shadow credit

For a reported unauthorised transaction, the bank provides a temporary (shadow) credit to the customer's account within 10 working days of the report while the investigation continues.

### Resolution timeline

The bank resolves the dispute within 90 days of the report. If the investigation finds the customer was negligent, for example by sharing the card PIN or a one-time password, the shadow credit is reversed.

## Failed ATM withdrawals

If cash is not dispensed but the account is debited, the bank automatically reverses the amount within 5 working days. If the reversal is late, the customer receives compensation for each day of delay.
